Version: 1.0.5
Date: 2026-11-12
  Features:
    - Restore the five optional Deadlock loader and beltbox tiers, with their existing item, recipe and research names, recipes and artwork.
    - Retain the Space Age turbo tier in the loader and beltbox upgrade chains.
    - With Space Age and transport belts enabled, vanilla turbo belts, underground belts and splitters can be crafted on any surface in assembling machines 2 and 3 and compatible fluid-capable modded assemblers; foundries remain supported.
  Compatibility:
    - Accept compatible original and Factorio 2.1 successor Deadlock providers; honor their independent loader and beltbox startup settings.
    - Supports Factorio 2.1 with original Matt's Library 0.7.0 or later, based on Matt's Logistics 0.2.0.
